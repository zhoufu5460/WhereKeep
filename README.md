### WhereKeep 隐私政策

WhereKeep（物品位置台账，以下简称「本应用」）是一款纯本地的个人收纳索引工具。我们高度重视你的隐私，本政策说明本应用如何处理（实际上是不处理）数据。

**1. 我们不收集任何数据**

- 无账号体系：无需注册、登录，我们不掌握任何用户身份信息
- 无埋点统计：不集成任何第三方分析 SDK，不记录使用行为
- 无广告、无内购、无订阅：本应用为付费买断制下载，不集成任何广告或支付 SDK
- 零系统权限：不申请相册、相机、麦克风、定位、通知、蓝牙、通讯录等任何系统权限，运行时不会出现任何权限弹窗
- 纯文字方案：本应用不拍照、不扫码、不获取位置信息，所有记录仅由文字与颜色标签构成
- 示例数据说明：首次启动时应用会在本地生成一条示例记录（如「冬被 → 卧室 → 衣柜顶层」），仅存于你的设备上，可随时删除

**2. 付费说明**

- 本应用为 App Store **付费买断制**下载：一次购买，解锁全部功能，无任何内购、订阅或后续收费
- 导出功能（CSV / JSON）永久免费，无任何付费墙
- 购买与支付由 Apple 处理，本应用不接触你的支付信息；购买记录由 Apple 掌握，与本应用无关
- 退款请通过 Apple 官方渠道申请（reportaproblem.apple.com）

**3. 数据仅存储在你的设备上**

- 你的位置层级（家/房间/容器）、物品记录、颜色标签与全部偏好设置（外观、背景花纹、界面语言、上次位置），仅以本地文件形式保存在设备上
- 本应用不进行任何网络请求，任何数据都不会离开你的设备
- 卸载本应用即彻底、不可逆地删除全部本地数据

**4. 导出说明**

- 导出功能在本机沙盒生成 CSV / JSON 文件，并通过 iOS 系统分享面板交由你选择的目的地（如存储到文件、AirDrop、邮件）
- 导出文件由你自行处置，本应用不保留副本、不上传任何内容

**5. 用途与合规**

- 本应用仅用于个人物品位置的记录、查找与导出，不包含任何用户生成内容的分享、社交或社区功能
- 不涉及任何估值、行情或金融建议

**6. 儿童隐私**

本应用不收集任何用户的任何数据，包括儿童。

**7. 政策变更**

若未来版本引入需要更新本政策的功能，我们将在本页面更新内容并标注新的日期。

## English

### WhereKeep Privacy Policy

WhereKeep (a location log for personal belongings, the "App") is a fully local home-indexing utility. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how the App handles (i.e., does not handle) data.

**1. We collect no data**

- No accounts: no sign-up or login; we hold no identity information
- No analytics: no third-party analytics SDKs, no usage tracking
- No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions: the App is a paid, one-time download with no ad or payment SDKs integrated
- Zero permissions: the App requests no system permissions (no photos, camera, microphone, location, notifications, Bluetooth, or contacts) and shows no permission dialogs
- Text-only design: the App takes no photos, scans no barcodes, and acquires no location — every record consists solely of text and color tags
- Sample data note: on first launch the App creates one sample record locally (e.g., "Winter blanket → Bedroom → Wardrobe top"), stored only on your device and removable at any time

**2. Paid app**

- The App is a **paid, one-time purchase** on the App Store: pay once, unlock everything — no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no future charges
- The export feature (CSV / JSON) is free forever, with no paywall
- Purchases and payments are handled by Apple; the App never touches your payment information, and purchase records belong to Apple, not the App
- For refunds, use Apple's official channel (reportaproblem.apple.com)

**3. Data stays on your device**

- Your location hierarchy (home/rooms/containers), item records, color tags and all preferences (appearance, background pattern, interface language, last used location) are stored as local files on your device only
- The App makes no network requests; no data ever leaves your device
- Uninstalling the App permanently and irreversibly deletes all local data

**4. Exporting**

- Exporting generates a CSV / JSON file inside the app's local sandbox and hands it to the iOS system share sheet, where you choose the destination (e.g., Files, AirDrop, Mail)
- Exported files are yours to manage; the App keeps no copy and uploads nothing

**5. Purpose and compliance**

- The App is solely for recording, finding and exporting the locations of personal belongings — it contains no user-generated content sharing, social or community features
- It involves no valuation, market or financial guidance of any kind

**6. Children's privacy**

The App collects no data from any user, including children.

**7. Changes to this policy**

If future versions introduce features requiring policy updates, we will update this page with a new date.

- Email: 297409398@qq.com
